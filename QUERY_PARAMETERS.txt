CHROVIEWER QUERY PARAMETERS

Values in the URL override locally saved viewer settings for that page load.

LIVE SOURCE
liveSource=ta                 Use TournamentAssistant live replay packets.
playerId=<platform id>        Platform ID whose live play should be watched.
hideUI=true                   Hide all cards, controls, settings, errors, and overlays.

AUDIO
masterVolume=0..1             Overall output level. Multiplies song and hitsound volume.
songVolume=0..1               Song volume before the master volume is applied.
hitsoundVolume=0..1           Hitsound volume before the master volume is applied.
hitsounds=true|false          Enable or disable hitsounds.
audioOffsetMs=-1000..1000     Shift song and hitsound timing in milliseconds.

For liveSource=ta, masterVolume is re-read while the viewer is running. A same-origin
overlay may update the iframe's query string with history.replaceState and the new
volume is applied without reloading the iframe, map, audio, or renderer.
Example: iframe.contentWindow.history.replaceState(null, '', '?liveSource=ta&playerId=123&masterVolume=0.4')

TA IFRAME SYNCHRONISATION
TA viewers on the same origin announce their platform ID, map hash, available replay
range, and playback clock to one another. Viewers with the same map hash rendezvous at
the earliest time available to every iframe and then correct clock drift automatically.
BroadcastChannel is used when available, with same-origin localStorage events as the
fallback. No parent-page coordinator is required.

LIGHTING AND PERFORMANCE
lights=full|static|none       Full mapped lights, fixed static lights, or no mapped lights.
lightshow=full-lightshow      Show only the full lightshow and hide gameplay objects.
graphicsQuality=none|low|medium|high
renderScale=0.5..1.5          Internal rendering resolution multiplier.

CAMERA
camera=static|follow|first-person
fov=60..120                   Replay camera field of view in degrees.

PLAYBACK AND SOURCES
autoplay=true|false           Attempt playback without waiting for the play button.
map=<BeatSaver key or URL>    Load a map preview.
replayUrl=<HTTPS URL>         Load a replay file.
scoreId=<ScoreSaber score>    Load a ScoreSaber replay.
beat=<number>                 Initial beat for non-live playback.
difficulty=<zero-based index> Difficulty index for a map preview.

EXAMPLE
?liveSource=ta&playerId=76561199016011545&hideUI=true&masterVolume=0.8&songVolume=0.7&hitsoundVolume=0.5&lights=static&renderScale=0.75
